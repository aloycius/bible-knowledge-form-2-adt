The seventh plague: Hail

Hail rained down in the form of fire. This was against “Nut”- Egyptian goddess of the sky. This plague was also against “Isis” the goddess of life and “Seth” the protector of crops. Again, warning is given before the enactment of the plague. Pharaoh was warned of the impending doom that will be faced if he does not listen to the Lord, and forget his own Egyptian gods and goddesses. Before the plague through Moses the Lord even warned Pharaoh to bring back all that is in the field, cattle and human beings alike. The plague came down in thunder, hail and fire (Exodus 9:13-35).

The eighth plague: Locusts

Locusts sent from the sky. This was a plague against “Seth” – Egyptian god of storms and disorder, and was also regarded as protector of crops. And the plague was also against Isis the god of life who had a head of locust. The eighth plague issued by the Lord had greater impact than the previous plague.
