The Ten Plagues: Revelation of God’s power

After Pharaoh refused to let the people of Israel go and offer sacrifice to the Lord, the land of Egypt was inflicted with Ten Plagues. The ten plagues are a series of catastrophic events through which the Lord humbled the Egyptians. On one side

God revealed Himself as the only and mighty God, and on the other side proved to the Egyptians that the gods and goddess they believed were not gods. Again, with these catastrophic events the Lord wanted to set free His people from slavery in

Egypt. It was a revelation of God’s love and compassion to His people Israel.

Number ten is a significant number in biblical numerology. It represents a fullness of quantity. The ten Egyptian Plagues means Egypt was completely plagued. Just as the “Ten Commandments” become symbolic of the fullness of the moral law of

God, the ten ancient plagues of Egypt represent the fullness of God’s expression of justice and judgments upon those who refuse to repent. Through the plagues,

Pharaoh was given an opportunity to let the Israelites go. Moses and Aaron were sent as messengers to Pharaoh, to instruct him to let the Israelites go so that they may serve the Lord freely. Each plague corresponds to each Egyptian gods and goddess.
