The call of Moses

Moses was called at the age of 80 as recorded in Exodus 7:7. He experienced his call through the unconsumed burning bush at Horeb, the mountain of God (Exodus 3:1-6). Moses’ encounter with the unconsumed burning bush is a typical manifestation of the fact that divine leadership is about serving the God of the universe. It was through this encounter that Moses became fully aware that God was present with him and that he was called to serve God’s people and purposes. Surprised by the unconsumed burning bush, Moses turned aside to see what that strange thing was.

As the Bible says, “And when the Lord saw that he turned aside to see, God called to him out of the burring bush ‘Moses, Moses …put off your shoes from your feet for the place you are standing is holy ground” (Exodus 3:1-6).

Moses was told to take off his shoes, this implies that he was told to leave behind his old ways and to be ready to accept God’s plan. The Lord revealed to Moses His plan to deliver the children of Israel out of their Egyptian bondage. God told Moses how the children of Israel were in great distress, affliction, and mourning because of their Egyptian taskmasters.
