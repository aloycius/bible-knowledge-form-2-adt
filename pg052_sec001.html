Application of statistics in the government

Statistics helps the government to make quick and appropriate allocation of social services. Again, statistical data are important in making governmental policies, and informing all administrative decisions. Statistics are used in the determination of the cost of living, for example, the government may decide to revise the employees’ payment scales in order to determine the standard of living. Furthermore, statistics are necessary in the preparation of the government or organization budgets, since it estimates or predicts the revenues and expenditures from various sources.

Application of statistics in the Church

On its part, the Church makes use of statistics for appropriate planning geared for effective provision of various social and spiritual services. For example, establishment of new parishes and dioceses depends on population statistics.

Statistics also helps the Church to determine the number of faithful in the local or worldwide Church. This can help to determine whether members increase or decrease. Moreover, statistics are used in the Church for proper allocation of pastors and other church servants in parishes, dioceses or other institutions where Church service is considered necessary.
