The main ideas in the book of Joshua are:

(1) God’s faithfulness in fulfilling His promises to Israelites by giving them the land of Canaan. (2) The book shows the importance of the covenant obedience and faith in God’s leadership. (3) The book presents the fact that leader’s obedience to

God results in divine help. (4) It emphasizes about God’s unwavering commitment to His covenant with the Israelites, bringing them into the land promised to

Abraham. (5) Furthermore, the book explains Joshua’s leadership. Joshua, as

Moses’ successor, led the Israelites in conquering and settling them in the Promised

Land, demonstrating both military strength and spiritual guidance. (6) The book gives a narrative on the consequences of both obedience and disobedience to God’s commands, demonstrating the need for faith and trust in God’s guidance. (7) The book records the Israelites’ conquest of Jericho, Ai and other cities, culminating in the division of the land among the twelve tribes. (8) It portrays God as a sovereign judge over all nations, emphasizing His power and authority, holiness and miracles.

(9) Another prominent idea in the book of Joshua is the fact that holiness and faith in God lead to miraculous events, such as the sun standing still at Gibeon. (10) The book emphasizes the importance of the covenant obedience before, during, and after

God fulfills His promises.

Importance of preparing leaders

Leadership is defined as the ability of an individual to lead, influence, or guide other individuals, teams, projects or institutions. In principle, leadership is goal oriented.

Joshua became successful in his leadership roles because he was prepared and empowered to be a leader. Therefore, it is vital to prepare and mentor young leaders.

The prepared and mentored leaders may bring significant change in their leadership.

Prepared leaders are more likely to succeed in achieving their roles and mission, gaining trust and respect, being able to create the spirit of teamwork, and demonstrating the spirit of hard work and humility among members of the organization. As a leader and Moses’s successor, Joshua was prepared by God and nurtured by Moses.
