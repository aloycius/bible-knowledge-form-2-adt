Pharaoh introduced the second assault which was the intensification of forced labour.

When the first assault failed, the king intensified the Hebrews’ labour, but the more they were oppressed the more they increased.

The third assault was killing of the male children of Hebrews at birth stool (Exodus

1:15-20). The king having failed to ruin the blessings by oppressive labour, he instructed the midwives to Hebrew’s women named Shiphrah (beauty) and Pua

(splendour)” to kill all Hebrews’ male children at a birth stool. But since the midwives were God fearing, they refused to commit murder. Due to this, God blessed the midwives with families. The other assault introduced by Pharaoh to oppress the

Hebrews, was casting into the Nile River all Hebrews’ male children born alive

(Exodus 1:22).

As explained above, forced labour aimed at wearing down Israelites to make them not beget children. By becoming tired men would not be productive and thus Israelites’ depopulation. By forced labour some people would die hence reduce the number of

Hebrews. The killing of baby boys was engineered by the prophecies of Egyptians’ prophets who predicted the birth of the liberator of the sons of Israel. In order to destroy the birth of the liberator the king had to set genocide plot against baby boys.
