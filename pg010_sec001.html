Preface

This textbook is the second in a series of four textbooks to Bible Knowledge for

“O’Level” Secondary Education, Form One to Form Four. The Bible Knowledge for

Form Two Secondary Education student’s textbook has been written following the

2023 Bible Knowledge Syllabus for Form Two students in the United Republic of

Tanzania issued by the Ministry of Education Science and Technology.

Structurally, the book consists of five chapters, namely; God’s Love and Compassion, the Sinai Covenant, Statistics and Census, Leadership, and Patriotism. Each chapter contains activities and exercises. You are advised to read comprehensively while employing essential reading techniques. You are encouraged to do all the activities and exercises, as well as other assignments that you will be tasked to carry out.

You must equally know that, these learning activities, exercises and assignments, provide students with an opportunity to learn, practice and demonstrate that they have achieved the intended learning goals.

The essence of this textbook is to help the student to obtain and develop the expected competencies. However, for excellent performance and for the acquisition of biblical knowledge and competencies expected from the study of Bible Knowledge, you are required to thoroughly read the Bible (Revised Standard Version) as directed by the syllabus.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
