The journey through the desert

The people left the Sea of Reeds to the wilderness of Shur. They walked for three days and found water. The journey in the wilderness was the first test of God to His people. God put human beings in the position to show their loyalty (Exodus 15:22).

Bitter water at Marah

When they arrived at Marah the Israelites found bitter water that they could not drink. Here the people murmured against Moses saying ‘what shall we drink?’

When Moses cried to the Lord, He showed him a tree and he threw it into the water, and the water became suitable to drink. The murmuring of the people was a sign of distrust and unfaithfulness; they had no strong faith. Because of the challenges they experienced, they quickly forgot the mighty works God did in Egypt and at the Sea of Reeds (Exodus 15:23-26).

From Marah the Israelite’s journey took them to Elim, where they found seven palm trees and twelve springs of water. Israel camped by twelve springs (Exodus. 15:27).

Miraculous food from heaven

In the wilderness of Sin, the people murmured again against Moses and Aaron for the lack of food, actually the murmuring was against God. In response to their murmuring the Lord sent manna and quail to feed them (Exodus 16:1-36).
