In the wilderness of Sin people murmured for lack of food, this was a sin of distrust.

Lack of appreciating God’s love and compassion leads to disobedience to God’s order. In the wilderness of Sin, the people were given miraculous food and were given instructions as regards manna. First not to preserve it till morning and nothing would fall on the Sabbath. But some people left manna till morning and it developed maggots, and others went to collect on the Sabbath, however they found nothing.

As the consequences of not appreciating God’s love and compassion, people focus on actions of men instead of the processes of God. The people of Israel now and then complained to Moses for their needs. They did not pray to God, always turning to Moses and complained as if the whole process of taking them out of bondage in

Egypt was the work of Moses. For example, at Rephidim people murmured against

Moses for lack of water.

Failure to appreciate God’s love and compassion may lead to punishment. When the people of Israel lacked patience at Mount Sinai when Moses delayed coming down from the mountain, they made a golden calf and worshiped it. God punished the offenders, Moses made them drink water mixed with the ground golden calf, and people died of undefined sickness. Others were killed at the spot by the Levites.

In the Book of Numbers people did not appreciate God’s love and compassion.

They complained for lack of water, because of their lack of faith, the Lord sent fiery snakes who bit people and they died (Numbers 21:6-9). This was punishment for lack of appreciating God’s love and compassion. The people who were above 20 years who came out of Egypt did not enter into the Promised Land. This punishment was for lack of appreciating God’s love and compassion.

Wandering in the desert for forty years was a punishment for not appreciating God’s love and compassion. Moses himself was punished, though he had a privilege of seeing the Promised Land at a distance, he could not enter it because he failed to glorify God at the waters of Meribah (Numbers 20:2-13). In short, the consequences of not appreciating God’s love and compassion are sin and punishment.
