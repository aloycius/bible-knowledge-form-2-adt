Spying out the land of Canaan

Spying the land of Canaan was a field work survey aimed at collecting information about the land. Observing God’s instructions, Moses sent twelve men, one from each tribe of Israel to survey the land of Canaan that God was about to give them. The spies were tasked to scout the land, its people and resources to assess its suitability for Israelites to inhabit it. Exploring the land of Canaan was an important event in the history of Israel (Numbers 13:1-2). The event marked a pivotal moment in the

Israelites’ journey from Egypt to the Promised Land, as it reflected both the faith and the failures of God’s chosen people. Not only that but also, the spying of the land presents the idea of collecting data for statistics, this is according to the book of

Numbers. It is a kind of field work research for the purpose of collecting first-hand information.
