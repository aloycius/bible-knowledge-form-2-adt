The exodus event: Difficulties on the way to the Promised Land and God’s providence

The exodus event implies the movement of the Israelites from Egypt to the Promised

Land. Pharaoh grip to the enslaved Hebrews was loosened by the tenth plague. In this part of discussion, you will be travelling with the sons of Israel from Egypt on the way to the Promised Land. The journey to the Promised Land revealed God’s love and compassion to His people. However, it revealed unfaithfulness of the people of Israel to God’s love and compassion.

The Israelites departed from Egypt and took a route of the desert to the mountain of the Lord -Sinai. The first stage of the journey was from Rameses to Succoth. After the Hebrews left this first campsite, the Lord attended them in a cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night (Exodus 13:20–22), revealing His love and compassion to

His people.

Threats from Egyptians

After Pharaoh let out of Egypt the sons of Israel, he changed his mind. So, he mounted his chariots and army to pursue the Israelites. When the Israelites saw the

Egyptians coming, they were terrified and murmured against Moses and the Lord.

However, Moses encouraged the people and he told them, the Lord would fight for them “Fear not, stand firm, and see the salvation of the Lord which he will work for you today, for the Egyptians whom you see today, you shall never see them again.

The Lord will fight for you and you have only to be still” (Exodus 14:13-14). “Then the angel of God who went before the host of Israel moved and went behind them; and the pillar of cloud moved before them and stood behind them” (Exodus 14:19).

Crossing the Red Sea and destruction of Egyptian army

The Red Sea is a misspelt form of the sea of reeds. Moses stretched out his hand over the sea and the Lord drove the sea back by strong East wind all night and made the sea dry and the people of Israel went into the midst of the sea on dry ground. The

Egyptians pursued the Israelites and the Lord made the water come back and killed the Egyptian army (Ex 14:21-29).
