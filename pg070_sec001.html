Patriotism according to the book of Joshua

Patriotism is a broad term that refers to: special affection for one’s nation; a sense of personal identification with the nation; special concern for the well-being of the nation; and, willingness to sacrifice to promote the nation’s good. Rightly put, patriotism is loyalty and a feeling of pride toward one’s nation or country. There is little difference between love and affection for one’s nation, and these terms are often used interchangeably. Furthermore, patriotism may involve feelings of love and devotion in national achievement, support for national values and willingness to contribute to the one’s nation development. However, the state of patriotism should not be used to promote ethnicity, culture, religious radicalism, or social division, instead it must be used as a call for true statesmanship and loyalty to the nation’s good.

In the book of Joshua, patriotism is seen in relation to national identity and loyalty to God’s covenant. Joshua was loyal to God’s covenant and led the people according to God’s guidance (Joshua 23).
