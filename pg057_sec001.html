Information to be collected from the land of Canaan

When Moses sent the spies to explore Canaan, he gave them guidance outlining the types of information (data) that were to be gathered, he instructed them saying; “Go through the Negeb and then into the mountain region, see how the land looks like, and whether the people living there are strong or weak, few or many. Spies were also tasked to measure whether the land they were about to occupy was good or bad.

They were equally assigned to see whether their cities have walls around them or not? The survey was above all intended to examine whether the soil was rich or poor, again, they were tasked to explore whether the land had trees or not”? Furthermore, the spies were supposed to bring evidence over the nature of the land. As they were instructed; “Do your best to bring back some fruits from the land” (Number 13:1-2).

Therefore, the men explored the land from the desert of Zin to the border of Hamath.

They went through the Negeb and came to Hebron where Ahiman, Sheshai, and

Talmai lived. These people were the descendants of Anak. The spies further advanced to the Eshcol Valley, where they cut off a branch with only one bunch of grapes on it. The two spies carried it on a pole between them. They also brought some pomegranates and figs. From that incidence, they called that valley Eshcol (Bunch of Grapes) because of the bunch of grapes the Israelites cut off there.

The spies’ report

After forty days of land mapping, the spies brought back the report. Ten spies though agreed on the beauty and potentiality of the Land, as they said “indeed the land flows with milk and honey,” yet, they did not agree that they would take over the land because of the nature of the people of the land since they were too strong to fight them, and their cities were fortified. They further reported that, they even saw the descendants of Anak, who were giant people, when compared to them; Israelites looked like grasshoppers.
