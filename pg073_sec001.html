Bravery involves facing fear and doubt, but not in a reckless or arrogant way, but rather with faith and reliance on God. Biblical examples of bravery often involve obeying God’s commands and remaining faithful, even when it is difficult. God told

Joshua to “Be strong and courageous” as he led the Israelites into the Promised Land, emphasizing God’s presence and support. There are many indicators of bravery and strength in the book of Joshua:

(1) God’s command to Joshua to be courageous and brave. Joshua accepted and practiced this in his mission. This is a typical indicator of strength and bravery.

Joshua was supposed to rely on these virtues. In this context, strength extends beyond the battlefield. It demands bravery to make difficult decisions, to stand firm in one’s convictions, and to trust in a divinely ordained path yet without being worried with uncertainty (Joshua 1:6-9). (2) Another indicator of strength and bravery in the book of Joshua is the fact that Joshua accepted and fulfilled a difficult mission. Leading the people of Israel was not an easy task. The book of Joshua, therefore illustrates that true strength and bravery often involves stepping into roles or situations that challenge our comfort zones, and push us toward growth. This kind of strength and bravery is rooted in faith, serving as a reminder that belief in a higher purpose can empower individuals to evidently overcome the unconquerable obstacles (Joshua

1:9). (3) The book of Joshua indicates that strength and bravery are necessary in making difficult decision. This means that, to stand firm in one’s convictions, and to trust in the divinely ordained path without being worried about uncertainties as demonstrated by Joshua is not easy. Joshua followed God’s instructions and invited the people of Israel to stand steadfast in their faith to the true God (Joshua 23-24). (4)

Furthermore, the book of Joshua indicates strength and bravery by showing Joshua heroically leading the Israelites to take over Canaan. With God’s providence Joshua surmounts the kings of Canaan. (5) Another indicator of strength and bravery in the book of Joshua is seen when Joshua took decision, and supervised the division of the land of Canaan among Jewish with such precision (Joshua 13-22). (6) Joshua in his farewell, admonished the people of Israel to stand firm in their faith to God, in case they go astray, on his part and his family will stand for God (Joshua 23-24).
