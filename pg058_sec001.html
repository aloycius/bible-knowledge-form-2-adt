Census

Census is defined as the overall process of collecting, processing, tabulating, analysing and publishing the demographic, economic and social data of the population and housing units in a specific country or part of it at a specific period of time. The term census is derived from the Latin word censere meaning ‘to assess’. The census process involves analytically collecting, recording, and calculating population information usually displayed in the form of statistics. Census is commonly used in getting national population and housing data. Furthermore, census is used in collection of agricultural information, traditional culture, business supplies, and traffic information. It is usually recommended that national population censuses be taken at least after every ten years.
